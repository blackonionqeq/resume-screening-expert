# Software Engineer — Backend Screening Criteria

## Required (must have)
- 3+ years of backend development experience
- Proficiency in at least one of: Node.js, Python, Go, Java, Rust
- Experience designing and building RESTful or GraphQL APIs
- Working knowledge of SQL databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, etc.)

## Nice to Have
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- Familiarity with containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)
- Microservices or distributed systems experience
- Open source contributions

## Disqualifiers
- Zero programming experience
- Purely frontend background with no backend exposure

## Scoring Guide
- 90–100: Exceptional, fast-track to interview
- 70–89: Strong fit, proceed to interview
- 50–69: Partial fit, discretionary review
- Below 50: Does not meet minimum requirements
